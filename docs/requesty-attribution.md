# Requesty referral attribution

The workspace footer links to the user-supplied Requesty invitation. The attribution is separate from grading and does not add model requests or change availability, routing, retention, keys, or budgets.

- Destination: https://app.requesty.ai/join?ref=b1f2b1e1
- Attribution: “AI feedback by Requesty.”
- Slogan: “Understand the mistake. Learn the word.”
- Visible disclosure: “Referral link. Signups may earn us AI credits.”
- External link: new tab, an accessible new-tab announcement, and `sponsored noopener noreferrer` with a no-referrer policy.
- Asset: `public/requesty-logo.png`, self-hosted rather than fetched from Requesty on each page view.

## Brand asset provenance

The Requesty wordmark comes from the [official Requesty website](https://www.requesty.ai), which references [this logo PNG](https://www.requesty.ai/logo_requesty_dark.png). The asset was trimmed and resized, with the white wordmark changed to the app's dark text color for a light background; the blue symbol and white terminal glyph are preserved. The Requesty trademark and brand asset remain their owner's property and are not relicensed under this app's MIT code license.

## Scope

This is a local-source and existing private-preview change, not a production release. No GitHub/GHCR write, release, database change, or Requesty organization setting change is included.
