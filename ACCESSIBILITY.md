# Accessibility

vocabulary.training is for practising words by typing, and it should be usable by everyone,
including people who use a keyboard only, a screen reader, zoom, or high-contrast and reduced-motion
settings.

## Commitment

We aim to meet [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) level AA. That is a goal, not a
claim of conformance: the app has not been through a formal accessibility audit.

## Supported environments

The current versions of the major evergreen browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on desktop
and mobile. Assistive technology is not tested systematically yet.

## Known limitations

No complete list exists yet, because there has been no audit. Barriers that are found are tracked
as issues with the `accessibility` label, and fixing them takes priority over new features.

## Reporting a barrier

[Open an issue](https://github.com/GothenburgOffice/vocabulary-training/issues/new?template=accessibility.yml)
saying what you were trying to do, the page, and your browser and assistive technology. If you
can't use GitHub, contact the maintainers through the
[GothenburgOffice organization](https://github.com/GothenburgOffice).
