# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0), in a shorter form for a small project.

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith, including when disagreeing;
- give and accept feedback on the work, not the person;
- stay on topic and keep the maintainers' limited time in mind (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other
people's private information, sexualised language or attention, and anything else that would be
out of place in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't
follow this. They decide what is appropriate, and a decision is explained when it is safe to do so.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request, or contact the maintainers
through the [GothenburgOffice organization](https://github.com/GothenburgOffice) on GitHub. Reports are
handled privately and in good faith. Security vulnerabilities go through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/GothenburgOffice/vocabulary-training/security/advisories/new),
not here.

This applies in project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
